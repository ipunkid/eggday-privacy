Support for EggDay - Habit Tracker / 蛋记

Last updated: September 10, 2026

English

Contact Support

If you need help with EggDay - Habit Tracker, send an email to the support address below. Please include your device model, iOS or watchOS version, and a short description of the issue.

mail@punkid.org

Data and Accounts

EggDay - Habit Tracker does not require an account or cloud service. Check-in records and settings are stored locally on your device.

Notifications

Daily reminders are local notifications. You can change notification permission in the iOS Settings app.

In-App Purchase

The Landmark Pass is a one-time purchase handled by Apple App Store. Use Restore Purchase in the app if you need to restore access.

Privacy Policy

Read the privacy policy at https://ipunkid.github.io/eggday-privacy/.
